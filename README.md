# Team-14---Mountain-Hut

Short motivation about License choosen:
We chose the European Union Public Licence v1.2 because it protects the openness of our project while keeping it easy to integrate with other software. Since our target distribution area is the European Union, where the Alpine huts are located, the EUPL also gives us legal certainty: it is based on EU law, it is valid in all official EU languages, and it clearly defines the applicable law and court.

We encourage huts to adapt the software to their own needs. However, if they distribute a modified version or offer it as an online service, they must release it under the same licence and make its source code publicly available. This way, every improvement benefits us, the other huts and the whole community.
